Quick Answers — Common Daily Questions
💵
When does the customer have to pay?
Underground service (~$3.28/ft), padmount jobs, temp power up/down, line extensions beyond ~600 ft, relocations. OH residential is usually free.
🔌
Who runs the conduit on a commercial UG job?
The customer runs ALL conduit from building to pole. At least one stick up the pole. Entergy provides standoffs and makes the final connection.
📐
Who pours the transformer pad?
The customer pours the pad to Entergy's UG-UPADT-POUR specification. Entergy provides and installs the transformer.
⏱️
How soon do I have to contact the customer?
Within 48 hours of job assignment. Phone first. Leave message + email if no answer. Document date and time in Maximo and Jira.
🪛
When do I need to run PoleForeman?
Framing changes, adding equipment, pole replacements on complex jobs, NJUNS attachments, line extensions with angles, any structural concern.
🕳️
What does boring cost and who pays?
~$26/ft. Customer pays if existing driveway blocks the UG route. Alternative: customer installs 3" sleeve conduit themselves for Entergy to slide 2.5" conduit through.
📦
What is an INTD and how do I avoid one?
Customer called the call center instead of you — meaning you lost communication ownership. Avoid by staying ahead of the customer with proactive updates before they have to ask.
🔢
PULLCICA2U-3 — what's the quantity?
Footage only. Do NOT multiply by 3. This CU already includes all 3 phases. Common mistake — double-check before estimating.
🌿
Who is responsible for vegetation clearing?
Entergy handles vegetation on OH lines. Use 1MHCMOH-VEG CU (qty = manhours) for cue template jobs. Note vegetation requirement at intake.
💰
Free line extension allowance — how far?
~600 ft from nearest source. Beyond that, CIAC applies based on construction cost minus estimated annual revenue credit. Never give ballpark estimates — use the standardized cost sheet.
